## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated to Claude and confirmed through the plan in `sites/081-Central Motos/arquitetura.md`
(2026-10-07): public storefront as static HTML generated by `build.mjs` from Supabase data
(Felipe's static pipeline), admin panel in Next.js 16 + Supabase (sa-east-1), both on Vercel.
Chosen after measuring the same page on Next 16 (mobile Lighthouse 94-96) versus without the
framework scripts (99), because PageSpeed 100 on mobile is a standing requirement.

## Users

- **Buyer:** people from Irecê, Luís Eduardo Magalhães and the surrounding region (interior of
  Bahia), almost always on a phone and often on 4G. They arrive from Instagram
  (@centralmotoslem), ads, links shared on WhatsApp and referrals. They want to know whether the
  monthly installment fits their budget and whether the bike is trustworthy, then talk on
  WhatsApp. Stock is popular street and trail bikes (Pop, Biz, CG, Bros, Factor, Fazer, XRE,
  Lander), new (0 km) and used.
- **Store staff:** the owner of Central Motos and the store team. They manage stock in the admin
  panel, most likely from a phone inside the store, taking photos on the spot.

## Product Purpose

A storefront for the stock, with the installment front and center and one tap to WhatsApp, plus
an admin panel where the store can register, price, photograph and publish bikes without touching
code. Success means more WhatsApp conversations per bike, stock that is always current, and a site
that is fast on a phone.

## Positioning

Financing 100% online with approval in 10 minutes; buy, sell and trade (the customer's used bike
counts as down payment); two stores in the interior of Bahia. The site shows the installment
instead of the full price.

## Operating Context

- Two stores share one stock list (decided 2026-10-07):
  - Irecê: R. Antônio Carlos Magalhães, 41, CEP 44860-069. WhatsApp likely (74) 99993-6265
    (area code 74 is Irecê).
  - Luís Eduardo Magalhães (LEM): address to be confirmed. WhatsApp likely (77) 98150-3989, the
    number in the Instagram bio (area code 77 is LEM).
- Sales close on WhatsApp. The site does not sell, and it does not ask for a name or phone before
  WhatsApp: one click opens the chat with the bike already in the message, and only the click
  count is recorded (decided 2026-10-07).
- The full price never appears on the site (owner's request). The 48x installment is calculated
  with no down payment from the private price, using a coefficient the owner edits in the panel
  (his reference points: 10k financed is about 48x 500 to 600; 20k is 950 to 1,100; 25k is 1,300
  to 1,400). When the bank quotes a different value, the owner types that bike's installment by
  hand (decided 2026-10-07).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Public site: home (approved mockup), stock page with filters, search, sorting and pagination,
  one page per bike, privacy policy.
- Admin panel: login, dashboard, create/edit/archive/delete bikes, draft → published → reserved →
  sold, highlight, manual order, multiple photos (order, cover, crop focus, delete), preview,
  settings (stores, WhatsApp, coefficient), delivery photos.
- PageSpeed 100 on mobile and desktop, plus SEO and GEO for Irecê and LEM (Felipe's standing
  quality rule).
- Supabase free plan in São Paulo: 500 MB database, 1 GB files, pauses after 7 days without
  activity.
- Undecided (do not invent): LEM address, which WhatsApp belongs to which store, owner's name,
  opening hours, FAQ answers (negative credit, which banks, down payment), Google rating, domain.

## Product Principles

1. The installment is the price: always visible, honest ("simulation, subject to credit
   approval"), never the full price.
2. One tap to WhatsApp, with the bike already in the message.
3. Truthful stock: a sold bike leaves the storefront; photos and data are real.
4. Fast on a real phone: the buyer is on 4G in the interior.
5. The store publishes on its own, from a phone, without help.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

language for a broad audience with little digital familiarity, and usable on a basic Android
